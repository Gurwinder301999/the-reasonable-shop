    How this works

    1 · Pick a channel

    Playback happens in your browser, straight from the broadcaster — their YouTube live channel or their own public HLS stream. Nothing is re-hosted here.

    2 · Streams are live-resolved

    Broadcasters rotate their live video IDs, so the server resolves the current live stream for a channel and the player swaps to it automatically.

    3 · Source switching

    Every player lists all available sources (YouTube / direct HLS / official site) so you can hop to whichever one works best on your network.

    4 · Kept honest

    Pay-TV channels are intentionally absent: their free streams in the wild are unauthorised restreams. Free-to-air, public-service and broadcaster-published streams only.
